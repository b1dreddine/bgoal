What BGoal looks like

Each screen is drawn from the app's Flutter code: the same layout, text, colours (Material 3, pitch-green theme) and spacing. They are mockups, not screenshots, because the app hasn't been compiled yet. Club and league crests are the real images from football-data.org, the same ones the app loads automatically for every team. Ad slots are shown where the app places them, with grey example ads.

Example data: matches, minutes and standings are invented for the pictures. In the real app they come live from football-data.org through your backend. The app shows each crest automatically from the address the data provider gives for the team, and caches it on the phone. If an image is missing or fails to load, or the app is built with --dart-define=SHOW_CRESTS=false, it shows the team's initials in a circle instead. Exact fonts follow the phone: Roboto on Android, San Francisco on iPhone.
